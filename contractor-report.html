        Before you act on this

        • Check every figure against your own estimate. We read your documents carefully, but you are the one signing, and a figure we misread is a figure you would be quoting back to someone holding the original.
        • A published national range is not a quote for your house. Where a finding rests on one, it says so. Treat it as a question to ask, and get a second bid on identical scope.
        • Statutory references are to the law as we understand it today. Local ordinances can add to it. Your building department will answer a permit question for free.
