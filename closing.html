What this does not do

Read this before you upload.

  • We do not say a fee is too expensive. Nobody publishes what an underwriting or settlement fee should cost, so we check arithmetic and federal rules instead of guessing.
  • Not every check runs on every closing. A document-only audit runs eighteen of twenty-seven. Your report says which ran, which were blocked, and which did not apply.
  • We read what you send. Lines we could not read are named in the free scorecard, before you pay.
  • Automated analysis, not legal or mortgage advice. Every finding shows its arithmetic or names its rule, so you can check it and take it to your lender or attorney.

Questions

Does this work for a refinance?
Yes. Twenty-six of the twenty-seven apply identically. Only the contract check is skipped.
What documents do I need?
Only your final Closing Disclosure. Loan Estimates and contract are optional and free.
Does this work in my state?
Yes. All twenty-seven run on your document's arithmetic or on federal rules — identical in all fifty states.
Why not just tell me if my fees are high?
Because we would be guessing. Nobody publishes what an underwriting fee should cost, and calling a legitimate charge excessive three days before closing is worse than saying nothing.
How is this different from reading the document myself?
Reading tells you what you are charged, not whether it is right. The APR, the finance charge, the escrow cushion and the tolerance caps only fail when you recompute them.
Will you contact my lender for me?
No. Every finding names the charge, the amount and the rule behind it, so you can raise it yourself. Not a law firm, not legal advice.
What if you find nothing?
The scorecard shows the count before you pay. If the report misses what it promised, we refund in full.
