If a fee rose after your Loan Estimate, your lender may owe you the difference back.

Federal rules cap how far certain closing costs can move between your Loan Estimate and your Closing Disclosure. Go over the cap without a documented reason and the lender has to refund the difference. We test every fee against those caps, recheck every calculation on the document, and measure your escrow cushion against the federal limit. Buying or refinancing, any state. Before you pay anything, we tell you what we found and what we could not read.

The Closing Disclosure Audit provides automated analysis for informational purposes only and is not legal, tax, financial, or mortgage advice, and is not an appraisal, a title opinion, or a determination of your tax liability. Findings are stated as potential issues to raise, not as legal conclusions, and nothing in a report asserts that any lender, title company or settlement agent has acted improperly. Benchmarked figures are drawn from publicly published government schedules, statutes, rate filings and assessment records, each cited in the report with the date it was checked; those sources change and may be superseded. Projections of future escrow or tax amounts are estimates, not representations of what you will be charged.
