Add a subscription to track

No need to set a reminder date yourself. We automatically check in — every 60 days on active subscriptions ("still using this?") and every 30 days on paused ones. If you tell us what show you're waiting on, we check daily for its real next air date (via TVmaze) and move your check-in to that date automatically.

How your streaming data is tracked

We never log into your Netflix, Hulu, Disney+, or any other account — nothing here connects to them, and we can't see what you actually watch or what you're billed. Everything above comes from what you type in yourself.

The one exception: if you name a show you're waiting on, we check a free public TV schedule (TVmaze) once a day for its next air date and update your reminder automatically. No passwords, no bank link, nothing running in the background beyond that one daily check.
