What we check

Ten checks, run against every property you enter.

Each one is decided before any writing happens, by matching your answers against a held reference set — so the same property produces the same finding every time.

01Registration and licensingDoes your city or state require a rental to be registered or licensed, and does this one hold it?
02Registration renewalCounted from the expiry date you give: lapsed, due inside ninety days, or clear.
03Federal lead disclosurePre-1978 housing carries a federal disclosure duty at every lease. Do you hold the signed copy?
04A child under six in an older unitThe fact pattern several states write specific duties around, over and above disclosure.
05State lead certificationSome states require an inspection or certificate for pre-1978 rentals. We name which, and who issues it.
06Periodic inspection programmesCities that inspect on a cycle rather than on complaint — so the visit is not a surprise.
07Documents that must go with the leaseWhere a city requires a summary or certificate attached to every lease and every renewal.
08How the security deposit is heldDeposits sitting in a personal account, in states that require them held separately.
09Notice window before a lease endsCounted from your own lease end date, so you know whether the decision is due this week.
10Permits for planned workRuns when you tell us about work coming up, including the lead-safe certification question on older buildings.
