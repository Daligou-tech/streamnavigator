What is StreamNavigator

We tell you exactly what to do. You make the change.

Streaming was supposed to save you money. Instead, most people are quietly paying for three or four services they forgot about or only need part of the year. StreamNavigator connects to your accounts, tracks what you actually watch, and shows you exactly where you're overpaying.

Then it tells you what to do about it — when to pause a subscription you're not using right now, and when to bring it back based on what that service actually has to offer, like a new season, a marquee release, or your team's season opener. Every recommendation comes with a direct link straight to that service's account page, so you make the change yourself in one tap.

  • ✓ A direct link to pause, downgrade, or cancel what you're not using
  • ✓ A reminder to bring it back, timed to what's worth watching
  • ✓ Instant alerts before a price hike hits your card
