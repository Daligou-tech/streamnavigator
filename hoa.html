What this does not do

Read this before you upload.

  • We read the documents you send, and nothing else. We have no access to the association's records, no live database, and no way to look it up. Anything the HOA never put in writing is invisible to us — and to you.
  • We do not tell you whether to buy. You get a risk read on the finances and the rules, plus the questions to ask. The decision is yours, and it depends on things no document shows.
  • A healthy association is reported as healthy. The report does not manufacture concerns to justify its price. If nothing is wrong, you have bought the confirmation — which is what you came for.
  • Estimates are labelled as estimates. A per-unit exposure figure is arithmetic on the association's own numbers, not a bill anyone has sent. We show the working so you can check it.
  • Missing documents are named, not guessed around. If the bylaws were not provided, the report says leasing could not be checked. It does not infer what they probably say.
  • Automated analysis, not legal or real-estate advice. Every finding cites the page it came from, so you can verify it and take it to an attorney or your agent.
